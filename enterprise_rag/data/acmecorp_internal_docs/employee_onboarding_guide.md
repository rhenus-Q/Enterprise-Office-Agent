# AcmeCorp Employee Onboarding Guide

- **Document ID:** HR-GDE-001
- **Version:** 6.2
- **Effective date:** 2026-03-01
- **Policy owner:** People Operations (people-ops@acmecorp.example)
- **Applies to:** All new employees and their managers

## 1. Purpose

This guide lists what new AcmeCorp employees should do before day one,
during their first week, and during their first 30 days, plus who to
contact when something is missing.

## 2. Before day one

- Sign your offer documents and complete identity verification in the HR
  portal at least **3 business days** before your start date.
- Your manager submits the onboarding ticket, which bundles your laptop,
  account provisioning, and a VPN access request (see the *VPN Access
  Policy*, ITSEC-POL-004 — no separate request needed).
- You will receive an Okta activation email on the morning of day one.

## 3. Day one

1. Pick up your laptop from IT (office) or confirm courier delivery
   (remote).
2. Activate your Okta account and enroll in **Okta Verify MFA** — required
   before any internal system, including VPN, will let you in.
3. Join the **#new-hires** Slack channel and say hello.
4. Meet your manager for the role expectations conversation.

## 4. First week checklist

- Complete **mandatory security awareness training within 7 days** of your
  start date (assigned automatically in the learning portal). Access to
  production systems is blocked until it is complete.
- Meet your assigned **onboarding buddy** — they are your first stop for
  "how does X work here" questions.
- Verify your VPN access works from your home network; problems go to the
  IT Service Desk (#it-helpdesk, ext. 4357).
- Enroll in benefits in the HR portal — enrollment closes **30 days** after
  your start date.
- Engineers: complete the dev-environment setup guide in the engineering
  wiki and make a first small merge request (docs fixes count).

## 5. First 30 days

- Finish role-specific training modules assigned by your manager.
- Have a **30-day check-in** with your manager and a separate one with
  People Operations.
- Engineers joining an on-call team: schedule your shadow rotation (see the
  *On-Call and Escalation Policy*, OPS-POL-003 — full rotation eligibility
  starts after 3 months).

## 6. Exceptions

Deadline extensions (training, benefits enrollment) must be requested
through your manager and approved by People Operations before the deadline
passes.

## 7. Contacts

| Need | Contact |
|---|---|
| Onboarding ticket status | people-ops@acmecorp.example |
| Laptop, accounts, VPN problems | IT Service Desk — #it-helpdesk, ext. 4357 |
| Payroll and benefits | benefits@acmecorp.example |
| Anything else | Your onboarding buddy or #new-hires |
