# AcmeCorp Expense Reimbursement Policy

- **Document ID:** FIN-POL-002
- **Version:** 5.0
- **Effective date:** 2025-11-01
- **Policy owner:** Finance Operations (finance-ops@acmecorp.example)
- **Applies to:** All employees submitting business expenses

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines which business expenses AcmeCorp reimburses, the
approval thresholds, and the submission process. It covers travel, meals,
equipment, software, and other out-of-pocket business costs.

## 2. Approval thresholds

| Expense amount (single item) | Required approval |
|---|---|
| Up to $100 | None beyond receipt — auto-approved in Concur |
| $100.01 – $1,000 | Direct manager |
| $1,000.01 – $5,000 | Department director |
| Over $5,000 | VP **and** Finance Operations review |

Expenses over **$500** should be approved *before* the purchase whenever
practical. Splitting a purchase into smaller items to stay under a threshold
is prohibited.

## 3. Receipts and submission

- Submit expenses in **Concur** within **60 days** of the purchase date.
  Late submissions require director approval and may be denied.
- Itemized receipts are required for any expense over **$25**, and always
  for lodging and airfare regardless of amount.
- Corporate-card transactions must still be submitted and coded in Concur
  within the same 60-day window.

## 4. Travel rules

- Book flights and hotels through the AcmeCorp travel portal (Navan)
  whenever available.
- Meal per diem: **$65 per day domestic**, **$90 per day international**.
  Per diem does not require itemized receipts.
- Mileage for personal-vehicle business travel is reimbursed at the
  prevailing IRS standard rate.
- Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is
  permitted on longer flights with manager approval.

## 5. Non-reimbursable expenses

- Commuting between home and a regular office.
- Traffic or parking fines.
- Alcohol beyond two drinks per person at a business meal.
- Personal entertainment, in-room movies, minibar items.
- Lost or stolen personal property.

## 6. Exceptions

Exception requests (late submission past 90 days, non-standard purchases,
threshold overrides) must be emailed to **finance-ops@acmecorp.example**
and approved by the Financial Controller. Document the approval in the
Concur report comments.

## 7. Payment timeline

Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next available payroll cycle —
typically within **2 pay periods** of final approval.
